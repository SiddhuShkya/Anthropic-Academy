# Being Specific

Adding guidelines or steps to steer the model's output in a particular direction.

## Two Types of Guidelines

| Type | Purpose | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Attributes** | List desired qualities/attributes of the output (length, structure, format) | Output *characteristics* |
| **B — Steps** | Provide specific steps for the model to follow in its reasoning process | *How* the model arrives at the answer |

## When to Use Each

- **Type A (attributes):** recommended for almost every prompt
- **Type B (steps):** use for complex problems where you want the model to consider a broader perspective or viewpoints it might not naturally weigh

Both types are often combined in professional prompts.

## Result

Adding guidelines took the meal-planning prompt's score from **3.92 → 7.86** — a significant quality jump.
